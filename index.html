Summary of Our Privacy Commitment

AirShare was built from the ground up on a fundamental privacy principle: your data belongs solely to you. AirShare does not use any cloud servers, databases, or analytics engines. All file discovery and data transfer occurs purely device-to-device across local hardware connections.

Zero Data Collection & Zero Servers

AirShare operates 100% offline between nearby Android devices using the Google Play Services Nearby Connections API. No files, contacts, device identifiers, or analytics are uploaded to any server or third party.

Specifically:

  • No Remote Servers: We do not operate, host, or communicate with any cloud servers, databases, or central repositories.
  • No User Tracking or Analytics: We do not integrate crash reporters, advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or usage analytics libraries.
  • No Accounts or Registration: You never need to sign up, sign in, or provide your name, phone number, or email to use AirShare.
  • No Intermediaries: Files are streamed directly point-to-point from sender to receiver without passing through external networks.

Permissions Used (On-Device Only)

To facilitate direct device-to-device wireless transmission without an internet connection, Android requires certain system permissions. All requested permissions are handled strictly on your local device:

Connectivity

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth & Location

Used strictly for local peer-to-peer device discovery and high-speed direct file transfer; location coordinates are never stored or transmitted.

Note on Android Location Permission: Android operating system policies require apps scanning for local Bluetooth low energy (BLE) and Wi-Fi Direct beacons to request Location access. AirShare never accesses, collects, logs, or shares your geographic coordinates or GPS location data.

File System

Storage / Media

Used only to read files you choose to send and save received files to your Downloads/ folder.

AirShare only reads files that you explicitly select for sharing through the system file picker. Received files are saved directly into your device's standard Downloads/ directory where you maintain complete access and ownership.

Underlying Technology

AirShare leverages the official Google Play Services Nearby Connections API to establish direct, high-bandwidth peer-to-peer Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connections between nearby Android devices.

The Nearby Connections API handles low-level cryptographic handshakes and device discovery locally. AirShare configures this connection in an offline-only mode, guaranteeing that file payloads transfer directly between device radios without routing through internet servers or Google infrastructure.

Children’s Privacy

Because AirShare does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data from any user, it does not collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or any other age group). The app is entirely safe and private for users of all ages.
